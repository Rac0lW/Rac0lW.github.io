Draw, check, adjust

Each round takes seconds, so you can repeat it until your hand learns the move.

  1. Look at the target

    Study the shape, or flash it for a few seconds to train visual memory.

  2. Draw it freehand

    Use Apple Pencil or touch. Pressure, multiple strokes and stroke stabilisation are supported.

  3. Read the feedback

    Get a score plus concrete notes, such as “the axis tilts 13°” or “the outline isn't closed”.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. Apple Pencil gives the most natural feel, but every exercise also works with your finger.

Is Draw Practice Lab free?

The app is free to download and the five basic shape exercises are free. A single one-time Full Version purchase unlocks every advanced exercise. There is no subscription.

Are my drawings uploaded?

No. Drawing analysis runs on your device and practice records stay on your device. The app has no account, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Does it use AI to judge my drawing?

No. Each attempt is measured geometrically against the target, so the same drawing always gets the same result and the feedback names what to change.

Which languages are supported?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
