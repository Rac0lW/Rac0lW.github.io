Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-10-16

English简体中文

English

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad App Store version of Draw Practice Lab, developed by Racol Wu. The separate browser version is outside this policy's scope.

The developer does not collect personal data through this app. Drawing analysis and practice records stay on your device. The app has no advertising, tracking or developer-operated analytics, and no account registration.

Drawing and practice data

The app processes strokes, scores, practice history, milestones and settings on your device. It does not send drawings or practice records to the developer. Reference models are bundled with the app. The app does not upload usage statistics or include a feedback submission form.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Version purchase through StoreKit. The app checks purchase entitlement with Apple and stores an unlock state locally so access can be restored. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password, and the app does not send purchase records to a developer-operated server. Apple handles purchase and transaction information under Apple's privacy policy.

Export, retention and deletion

You choose whether to export practice records or share an image. The system share sheet sends the selected file to the app or destination you choose; that destination handles it under its own policies. Local records remain until you clear them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not delete files you exported, copies in device backups or Apple's purchase records. Manage those copies through the relevant file storage, backup or Apple settings.

If you contact us

If you email racolw@163.com, the developer receives your email address and anything you choose to include. This information is used to answer your request and resolve the issue, and is retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may request deletion by email. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to explain the issue.

This policy webpage

This page uses no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in its Pages documentation and privacy statement. This website hosting activity is separate from using the installed app.

Children and policy changes

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information by email, contact us to request its deletion. If the app's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated.

Privacy contact: Racol Wu, racolw@163.com.

简体中文

本政策适用于 Racol Wu 开发的「绘画练习室」（Draw Practice Lab）iPhone 和 iPad App Store 版本，不涵盖独立的浏览器版本。

开发者不通过本应用收集个人数据。绘画分析和练习记录保留在你的设备上。应用没有广告、跟踪或开发者运营的使用分析服务，无需注册账号。

绘画与练习数据

应用在设备上处理笔迹、评分、练习历史、里程碑和设置，不向开发者发送画作或练习记录。参考模型随应用提供。应用不上传使用统计，也不包含留言反馈表单。

内购

可选的「完整版」一次性购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。应用向 Apple 核验购买授权，并在本机保存解锁状态，以便恢复使用权限。开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账号密码，应用也不会把购买记录发送到开发者运营的服务器。Apple 按照其隐私政策处理购买和交易信息。

导出、保存与删除

是否导出练习记录或分享图片由你决定。系统分享菜单会把选中的文件交给你指定的应用或保存位置，接收方按照自己的政策处理文件。本机记录保留至你清除记录或删除应用。删除应用会移除其本机数据，但不会删除已导出的文件、设备备份中的副本或 Apple 保存的购买记录；请通过相应的文件存储、备份或 Apple 设置管理这些内容。

主动联系我们

如果你向 racolw@163.com 发送邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及你主动提供的内容。这些信息用于回复请求和解决问题，仅在处理所需期间或适用的记录保存义务要求期间保留。你可以通过邮件请求删除。请不要发送与问题无关的敏感信息。

本隐私政策网页

本页没有分析脚本、广告或跟踪 Cookie。页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访客的 IP 地址，详见其 Pages 文档和隐私声明。网页托管产生的记录与使用已安装的应用分开处理。

儿童与政策更新

开发者不会有意通过本应用收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童通过邮件发送了个人信息，可联系我们请求删除。应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策及生效日期。

隐私联系人：Racol Wu，racolw@163.com。
